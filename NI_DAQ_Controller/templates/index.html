ℹ️ Device Detection

If NI DAQ devices are not detected, follow these steps in order:

  1. Refresh — Click Refresh in the toolbar to rescan for connected devices.
  2. Check wiring — Verify that each NI DAQ device is powered on and that USB or Ethernet cables are securely connected.
